01 — ABOUT

More Than a Cricketer

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer known for his exceptional batting, leadership and competitive spirit. He made his international debut in 2008 after leading India to the Under-19 World Cup title.

Over the years, he became one of the leading batters in world cricket and a powerful influence on India's cricket culture. His journey is a reminder that talent becomes extraordinary when it is combined with discipline and hard work.
